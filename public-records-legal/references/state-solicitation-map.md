# State Solicitation Map

**Status: not yet populated.**

This file holds, per state, the rules that limit using court or public records to solicit the people named in them. A row is added only after `scripts/verify_quotes.py` reports the quoted text as `found` at an official source and a person has read the provision. Research output that has not passed that check does not belong here.

## Row format

| State | Citation | Kind | Reaches | Lead types | What it restricts | Quote (verified) | Official URL | Verified on |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

- **Kind**: statute, court rule, administrative order, portal terms, or bulk agreement term.
- **Reaches**: `anyone` (a law of general application), `portal users`, or `licensees only`. This column decides most questions. A term that binds only licensees does not bind someone who never signed.
- **Lead types**: probate, eviction, foreclosure, divorce or family, other civil, code violation or lien.
